Machine Learning. Supervised learning is a machine learning approach that learns from labelled training data. Unsupervised learning finds structure without labels. Classification assigns discrete classes. Regression predicts continuous values. Precision is the fraction of retrieved instances that are relevant. Recall is the fraction of relevant instances that were retrieved. A neural network consists of layers of units that transform representations. Overfitting happens when a model memorises training examples. Cross validation estimates generalisation by holding out folds. Machine Learning.
