Lecture notes — Memory Management
Page 1
Paging divides memory into fixed-size pages. A page fault occurs when the needed page is not in RAM.
Replacement policies include LRU, FIFO and clock. Thrashing happens when the working set does not fit in memory.

Page 2
Segmentation divides address space into variable-sized logical units. Fragmentation can be internal or external.
Copy-on-write shares pages between processes until a write occurs.
