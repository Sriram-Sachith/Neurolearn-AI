Lecture notes — Probability
Page 1
A random variable maps outcomes of an experiment to numbers. Expectation is the probability-weighted average.
Variance measures spread around the mean. Independent events satisfy P(A and B) = P(A)P(B).

Page 2
Bayes' theorem relates posterior probability to likelihood, prior and evidence.
A Bernoulli trial has two outcomes. The binomial distribution counts successes in n independent trials.
The central limit theorem says sample means approach a normal distribution as n grows.
