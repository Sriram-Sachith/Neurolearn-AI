Lecture notes — Introduction to NLP
Page 1
Tokenisation splits text into words or subwords. A bag-of-words model ignores word order and counts tokens.
TF-IDF down-weights terms that appear in many documents. Word embeddings place similar words nearby in vector space.

Page 2
Named entity recognition labels people, places and organisations. A language model assigns probabilities to sequences of tokens.
Attention lets a model weigh relevant positions when producing an output.
Transformer encoders stack self-attention and feed-forward layers.
